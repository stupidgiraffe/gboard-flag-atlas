Gboard GIF and Sticker Prediction Flags Explained

Understand contextual GIF search, custom stickers, prediction timing and why an expression tab can differ across apps.

What you should know

Search-ranking controls, sticker prediction and UI tabs are separate from which GIFs a provider can return.

Evidence standard: these are real catalog identifiers, but their effects can be inferred, version-specific, or gated. Check the confidence and APK notes before changing settings.

Search suggestions versus content

enable_contextual_gif_search_query_suggestion suggests recommending a search query based on nearby context; enable_prioritize_recent_gifs suggests reordering results by recency. Neither guarantees that a GIF appears in every conversation. Availability can depend on provider data, connectivity and regional or app restrictions.

Sticker generation and presentation

enable_custom_sticker_tab and enable_sticker_predictions_while_typing point at surfaces for custom content and typing-time suggestions. The catalog also includes settings for delays and rendering, but those are different layers from whether a sticker is supported at all. An animation or chip appearing is not equivalent to successful sending.

Compare surfaces in the same conversation

Check the expression tab, search, and typed suggestions separately. Use the same text in two chat apps and record whether the result is suggested, selected and actually inserted. If an experiment merely rearranges a UI panel, capture the before/after position rather than claiming that GIF quality improved.

Frequently asked questions

Why do GIF results change without a flag edit?

Online provider inventory, recent usage and context can change independently.

Are custom sticker flags guaranteed to be available?

No. The visible identifiers do not establish product entitlement or supported generation backends.
