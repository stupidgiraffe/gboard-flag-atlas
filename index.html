How to use this atlas

  1. Search for a feature or browse a category.
  2. Open details to see the exact identifier and what is—and isn't—verified.
  3. Use Copy to paste a name into GboardWu → Patches → More settings → Developer options → Flag Editor.
  4. Change one setting at a time. Some changes require restarting Gboard; many experiments simply won't work on every version.

Why the caution labels?

A string inside an APK isn't proof that a feature is enabled, editable, safe, or available on your device. This catalog separates name-based interpretation from more specific research. Flags linked to AI models, networks, or audio may consume resources or affect privacy.
